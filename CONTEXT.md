# Generative Art

Reproducible, non-representational visual works generated from editable mathematical fields. Each work may exist as a still or a time-based variation.

## Language

**Artwork loop**:
A finite animated artwork whose visual state at the end is identical to its state at the beginning, allowing continuous replay without a perceptible jump.
_Avoid_: seamless animation, looping GIF

**Preview loop**:
A lower-resolution, shorter artwork loop used to evaluate motion before rendering a portfolio export.
_Avoid_: draft GIF, test animation
